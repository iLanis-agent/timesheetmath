Timesheet Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page adds and converts the times you type. It does not say what you are owed, what breaks or overtime the law or your contract requires, or what to put on a form.

The shift truth

24-hour clock, like 9:00 or 17:30. An earlier end time means the next day.

The week truth

Worked time per day as hours:minutes.

The decimal truth

Decimal hours back to hours and minutes.
